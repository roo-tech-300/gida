Local access, transport, and amenities

What is Gidan Kwano (GK) and where should you live?

Gidan Kwano, popularly called GK, is where the FUT Minna main campus is located. If you are in any faculty or school apart from SSTE, the best place to stay is in GK, as almost all of your classes will be held there.

Are there other areas to stay besides GK proper?

Yes. The name “GK” can sometimes be deceiving, because there are surrounding communities that are also great for students, such as Gidan Mangaro, Dama, and Talba Road. These are all conducive areas to live, but if you want to be as close to the school gate as possible, you should secure a place directly in Gidan Kwano — what students call living “front of school”.

What is the community like in Gidan Kwano?

Gidan Kwano features a vibrant mix of students, business owners, and indigenes.

How do you move around Gidan Kwano?

The primary modes of local transportation are kekes (tricycles) and motorcycles (bikes). You can take a bike or keke from anywhere to get to the “front of school”.

How do you enter the FUT Minna school grounds from Gidan Kwano?

Once you reach the front of the school and want to head inside the campus, you must use transport that is officially registered with the institution. You can easily recognise them by their purple-painted kekes, or bike riders wearing “FUT Minna” branded reflecting jackets.

What amenities are available around FUT Minna Gidan Kwano campus?

Gidan Kwano and the surrounding areas are well-equipped with student-friendly essentials, including:

  • Churches and mosques for worship
  • Viewing centres and game shops
  • Coworking spaces
  • Football pitches
  • General shops and commercial spots located all around the campus perimeter
Last checked: 30 September 2026. Keké and bike fares, and road conditions, can change with the season — confirm current rates locally.

Is Gidan Kwano the right area for you?

It may be a convenient option if your classes and regular activities are based at the main campus. Confirm your department’s current campus and compare the full journey—not just distance—before committing.
