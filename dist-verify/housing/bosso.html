About FUT Minna’s Bosso campus

FUT Minna has a campus at Bosso as well as its main campus at Gidan Kwano. The university identifies the School of Science and Technology Education (SSTE) at Bosso. Confirm your school’s current location and class schedule directly with the university.

Questions to ask about a Bosso-area home

  • How long does it take to reach your classes at the times you need to travel?
  • What transport options are available, and what do they cost?
  • Which utilities and facilities are included in the rent?
  • Who is responsible for maintenance and urgent repairs?
  • What are the occupancy, visitor, security, and payment terms?
