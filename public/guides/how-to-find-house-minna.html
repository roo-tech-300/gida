Create your account, find a place that fits, and secure your spot in five simple steps.

From signup to your spot

  1. 01

    Open Gida

    Visit gida.apartments and tap Get started.

  2. 02

    Create your account

    Enter your name, email, and password.

  3. 03

    Set your preferences

    Choose your campus area, yearly budget, and preferred room layout.

  4. 04

    Choose a lodge

    Browse listings, pick a place, then reserve solo or with roommate matching.

  5. 05

    Pay your share

    Review the checkout amount and pay to secure your spot.

After payment, Gida takes you to your booking or roommate lobby.
